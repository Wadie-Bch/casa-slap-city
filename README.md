# Casa Slap City

All of Casablanca runs after you to slap you. Slap them first.

**Play:** https://wadie-bch.github.io/casa-slap-city/

A top-down arcade game that streams the whole city of Casablanca (about 20 x 11.5 km, 44 quartiers, 160k buildings)
in the browser. Plain HTML5 canvas, no engine, no dependencies. Works offline once loaded.

## Install on iPhone (Safari)
1. Open the link above in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the home screen once while online and wait for "Ready offline" on the title screen.
   After that it plays with no internet.

## How to play
* Left thumb: move. Right thumb: **SLAP!** (tap once per slap; a miss leaves you open for a moment).
* Everyone chases you. Slapped people fly, get up angrier, and run away after enough slaps.
* Fill the bar for a **MEGA SLAP** that clears everyone around you.
* Aunties throw slippers: slap a slipper to send it back.
* Tea glasses give a heart back. New quartiers give bonus points.
* The magnifier button changes the zoom. The pin on the title screen picks the starting quartier.
* Keyboard: WASD or arrows, Space to slap, P to pause.
